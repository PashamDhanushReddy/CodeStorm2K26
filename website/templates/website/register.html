Registration Flow
  1. Fill in all team details (Team name, size, theme, and all member information)
  2. Click "Calculate Registration Fee" button
  3. Review your calculated registration fee (with discount if applicable)
  4. Scan the QR code and make payment
  5. Upload payment screenshot
  6. Click "Register Team" to complete registration
